About This Edition

Twenty Thousand Leagues Under the Sea was written by Jules Verne and first published in French in 1870. The English translation used as the basis for this edition was produced by anonymous volunteers for Project Gutenberg.

This is a modernized adaptation of Verne's classic novel. The story, characters, and scientific wonder of the original are preserved, but the prose has been reimagined in a contemporary voice -- clarifying archaic phrasing, tightening pacing, and making the narrative more accessible to today's readers.

The adaptation was produced with AI assistance (Anthropic's Claude), guided by a human editor who reviewed every chapter for fidelity to the source material. Footnotes throughout the text highlight significant departures from the original and explain the reasoning behind key changes.

The original text is in the public domain in the United States and is freely available from Project Gutenberg.
